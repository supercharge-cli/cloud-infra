# Billing

## Free base

Every account gets **one free workspace** at 2 vCPU · 4 GB. It does not expire. Add agents inside it.

## Paid sizes

| Size | India | International |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 vCPU · 4 GB | ₹199 / month | $2.29 / month |
| 4 vCPU · 8 GB | ₹399 / month | $4.59 / month |
| 8 vCPU · 8 GB | ₹599 / month | $6.79 / month |
| 16 vCPU · 16 GB | ₹1199 / month | $13.49 / month |

The dashboard always shows the current price before you pay.

## Monthly autopay

Paid sandboxes are monthly subscriptions through Razorpay. You approve a recurring payment once and each month renews automatically until you cancel.

- **Payment methods:** a card that supports recurring payments, or UPI Autopay / e-mandate approved in your UPI or bank app.
- **Start:** open the dashboard settings, pick a size in the **Billing** card and choose **Subscribe**. A new account can subscribe straight from **Create sandbox**.
- **Next charge:** shown on the Billing card.
- **Change size:** upgrades apply now, downgrades at the next renewal. UPI Autopay and e-mandate subscriptions cannot change size; cancel and subscribe to the new size.

## Cancel

Choose **Cancel subscription** on the Billing card and confirm. There are no further charges. The sandbox keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then it pauses.

## Failed payments

If a renewal fails, Razorpay retries it and the sandbox keeps working for a **3-day grace period** (the Billing card shows the date and a **Fix payment** link). If it still fails, the sandbox pauses.

## Paused for billing

A paused workspace keeps its files, settings and agent memory for **14 days**. Subscribing or paying the due amount in that time resumes it. After that it may be deleted. An upgraded free workspace goes back to the free base instead of pausing.

## Older one-off payments

Workspaces paid with a one-off 30-day payment keep their paid-until date. When it ends, the dashboard offers **Subscribe**. If you subscribe before it ends, the first autopay charge happens on that date, so you never pay twice for the same days.

## Refunds

Charges are not refunded; duplicate or erroneous charges are reversed. See the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refund), [Terms](/terms) and [Privacy](/privacy). For payment issues, contact `hello@vectra.cloud` with your account email and the Razorpay payment id.
