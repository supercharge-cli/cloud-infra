# Billing

## Free tier

Every account includes **1 free instance** (no payment, no expiry).

- Create it from the dashboard with **Create free instance**.
- Destroying a free instance frees the slot so you can create another free one later.
- Extra sandboxes beyond the free slot require payment.

## Price (paid instances)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| India | **₹** per instance (set by host; default ₹399) / **30 days** |
| International | **$** per instance (set by host; default $4.59) / **30 days** |

Each payment unlocks **one** additional sandbox for 30 days. Multiple paid instances = multiple payments.

## How payment works

1. On the dashboard, choose currency and click **Pay & create** (or **Renew** on an existing paid instance).
2. Complete checkout with Razorpay.
3. After a successful payment, access is active until the **paid until** date on the card.

## Renew

When a **paid** period ends, status shows payment expired. Use **Renew** on that instance — you keep the same sandbox and settings; only the paid period extends. Free-tier instances do not expire and do not need renew.

## Notes

- Free-tier sandboxes are provisioned immediately (no checkout).
- Paid sandboxes are not provisioned until payment succeeds.
- You can still **see** expired or paused instances on the dashboard and renew or resume as needed.
- **Refunds:** sales are final under our [Refund Policy](/refund). See also [Terms](/terms) and [Privacy](/privacy).
- For payment issues, contact support (`hello@vectra.cloud`) with your account email and approximate payment time.
