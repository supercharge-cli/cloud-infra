# Billing

## Price

| | |
| --- | --- |
| India | **₹** per instance (set by host; default ₹399) / **30 days** |
| International | **$** per instance (set by host; default $4.59) / **30 days** |

Each payment unlocks **one** sandbox for 30 days. Multiple instances = multiple payments.

## How payment works

1. On the dashboard, choose currency and click **Pay & create** (or **Renew** on an existing instance).
2. Complete checkout with Razorpay.
3. After a successful payment, access is active until the **paid until** date on the card.

## Renew

When the period ends, status shows payment expired. Use **Renew** on that instance — you keep the same sandbox and settings; only the paid period extends.

## Notes

- Sandboxes are not provisioned until payment succeeds.
- You can still **see** expired or paused instances on the dashboard and renew or resume as needed.
- For payment issues, contact support with your account email and approximate payment time.
